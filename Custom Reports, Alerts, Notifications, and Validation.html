Scope of response — Reports & operational visibility

Macquires understands that Syriatel needs more than static report definitions: supervisors and back-office teams must see live workload, SLA adherence, and exception queues while agents work in the same system. Our proposal therefore describes reporting that is native to the CRM—fed directly from telecom operations, payments, inventory, integration attempts, and audit tables created at the counter—not a separate data mart that lags behind the business day.

Macquires will provide showroom agents, back-office approvers, and management users with role-appropriate dashboards and filterable operational registers. Branch and regional scope, where configured, limits strategic views to the subscriber base each role may oversee. All figures shown in management MIS strips are traceable to underlying operation and payment rows, supporting audit questions without exporting to a third tool for routine supervision.

Standard operational reports, data tables & supervisor dashboards
Macquires will deliver the operational reporting pack below as part of the Syriatel CRM go-live. The pack includes live KPI strips (daily and configurable date ranges), supervisor cockpits, searchable operation registers with per-case audit trails, payment and integration logs, and executive MIS views on the unified dashboard.

Evaluators often ask for named reports such as a Daily Sales Report, an Active Suspensions Log, or a SIM Swap Audit Trail. For Daily Sales, Macquires treats new line activations and device sales as first-class operation types (ACT- and DEV- prefixes). Supervisors see completion rate, fallout to failed or pending-external status, average handling time against a five-minute activation SLA, and ranked rejection reasons—equivalent to a branch daily sales quality report, not only a raw count.

For Active Suspensions, the suspension panel reports volumes completed and failed, cases still awaiting back-office approval, fraud-related suspensions, and lines with auto-reconnect scheduled. The same data can be exported from the operations grid filtered by kind and status for a formal “log” extract at go-live if Syriatel requires a scheduled file.

For SIM Swap Audit, every completed swap writes an operational audit entry including old and new ICCID context (the “black box” snapshot). Lost or stolen swaps are counted separately in KPIs; identity documents remain linked to the operation for supervisor review before approval.

Integration success or failure is logged in parallel so IT and operations can reconcile CBS/HLR issues without a separate trouble-ticket spreadsheet.

Supervisor & management dashboards

Unified operator cockpit (persona-driven)

Macquires will configure the Syriatel unified dashboard (/Dashboards/DefaultDashboard) so each persona—Executive, Retail showroom, Back Office, Call Center, and system administration—sees only widgets relevant to their duties. Widgets are loaded dynamically from the platform dashboard service and refresh on a polling interval appropriate to operational use.

Retail users receive quick links and status tiles oriented to activation and customer search. Back-office users see pending approval counts. Executives with MIS permission see an embedded strip for ARPU and churn proxies plus branch workload heat, and may open full Strategic Analytics for revenue index trends, technical-ticket SLA, subscriber segment mix, and a branch leaderboard scoped by region or branch.

  • Bento cockpit — persona widgets via GET /Dashboard/GetMyWidgets
  • MIS strip — ARPU proxy, churn proxy, city/branch workload (GetTelecomDashboardKpis)
  • Strategic Analytics — executive KPIs with scope filters (GetStrategicMetrics); standalone page /Telecom/StrategicAnalytics

Back Office operations dashboard (supervisor control tower)

Macquires will provide a dedicated Back Office Dashboard that consolidates what would otherwise require dozens of separate reports. One screen gives Syriatel supervisors the live technical-ticket queue (open and in-progress, with critical-priority highlighting), financial performance for payment services, and KPI panels for every major lifecycle process: change GSM type, change number, package migration and VAS, termination, suspension, reconnect, refund, ownership transfer, SIM swap, selling-line activation, and device sales.

Each panel supports operational supervision: counts for the day (or selected period), completed versus failed, back-office pending approvals, SLA compliance percentages, and top reasons or offer names where applicable. Payment services additionally lists the latest payment transactions so supervisors can investigate failed or reversed PAY- rows without leaving the dashboard.

Macquires recommends this dashboard as the primary supervisor workstation for Syriatel back-office and team-lead roles, with the Telecom Hub used for drill-down into individual operations.

Telecom Hub — agent and team-lead workbench

Macquires will provide the Telecom Hub as the day-to-day operational register for showroom and call-center staff. The grid lists all operation types with status, document state, MSISDN, approval requirements, and flags such as lost-or-stolen SIM swap. Navigation badges show how many cases await back-office action, reducing the need for informal escalation channels.

Team leads use the same grid with filters to answer “what failed today on this branch?”—functionally equivalent to a lightweight operational report with full drill-down to operation detail, audit history, and attached identity documents.

Period reporting & KPI API catalogue

All major KPI endpoints accept optional from/to UTC dates (and branch where applicable). Macquires will use this capability during go-live to define Syriatel’s official daily, weekly, and monthly operational packs—e.g. end-of-day activation quality per branch—without building separate report engines for each period.

Available KPI domains include: selling-line activation, SIM swap, suspension, reconnect, termination, change number, change GSM type, takeover, offer migration and VAS, device sale, refund settlement, payment services, telecom dashboard MIS strip, and strategic executive metrics.

Platform context, go-live options & honesty note

The Macquires platform also includes ERP-style dashboards (sales, inventory, purchase, leads) for enterprises that enable those modules. For Syriatel, telecom operational reporting described above is in scope for the CRM rollout; ERP widgets remain optional.

Macquires will work with Syriatel during design workshops to confirm any additional named reports (fixed PDF layouts, regulator submissions) and implement them as scheduled extracts or SSRS/Power BI datasets on the same operational tables. Revenue and ARPU figures in MIS and strategic views currently use documented operational proxies until full CBS revenue feeds are connected; labels and tooltips will make this clear to management users.

Role-based access will be enforced: MIS and strategic analytics require telecom.reports.mis; billing and integration logs require dedicated telecom permissions; PII and national ID reveal remain audited.

Implementation note: Demo environments use mock CBS, HLR, and SMS connectors. Report structure, KPI definitions, and audit behavior are production-ready; only revenue depth and external gateway live traffic depend on Syriatel interface go-live.
